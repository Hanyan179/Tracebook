# Original generated scene assets

Created 2026-10-22 with the built-in image generation tool, not the API/CLI fallback. No reference images were supplied. No customer data, third-party photography or client screenshots were used. PNG masters were inspected before optimized PNG encoding; responsive versions are format/size optimizations of those originals.

- `desk-scene-v3.png` / `desk-scene-v3-small.png`: fictional editorial workspace at blue hour, charcoal wall with left-side headline space, thin unbranded laptop, phone and watch on walnut desk at right, warm window rim light, physically plausible aluminum/fabric, all screens off, no text/logo/UI/watermark. Wide 16:9 composition, understated warm-neutral palette.
- `walk-scene-v3.png` / `walk-scene-v3-small.png`: fictional early-morning river walk, adult forearm with woven watch strap at right, cropped hand/fingers, dark blank watch screen, layered river/bridge background and left-side text space. Natural editorial light, no text/logo/UI/watermark.

Prompt constraints: original fictional scenes, realistic material and geometry, no borrowed campaigns, no generated app interfaces, no implied live functionality. The source-card UI and real Tracebook mark are added separately in HTML. These assets are decorative marketing illustrations and are explicitly labeled as AI-generated scenes in both languages.

- `tap-scene-v4.png` / `tap-scene-v4-small.png`: generated morning doorway close-up of an adult holding a silver phone with its back toward the camera while a second-hand index finger contacts the center back. Warm-neutral editorial light and clean right-side space; no logo, UI or text. A targeted built-in image edit changed only the camera module to a small square triangular three-lens arrangement, retaining hand anatomy, touch point and lighting. The finalized 1280/640 px PNGs were inspected directly; no source photography was borrowed.
